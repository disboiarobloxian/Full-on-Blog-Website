Work at a Pizza Place

This game is made by Dued1 back in 2008. In this game, you get to be an employee in a pizza place and serve
customers their orders. You can either be a cashier, a cook, a boxer, a deliveryman
or a supplier, each roles has their specific tasks and they are very simple

The Jobs

The tasks in this game are very simple and doesn't need that much skill
to do, it offers a relaxing pace for casual players and if you do however
don't do much work, then there isn't much any penalty other than a reduce pay
but that doesn't really matter because your pay is based of off how much work
you put in a single day.

Relaxing Game

This game also gives players a choice to relax or to mess with other people
because believe me there are people that has nothing else to do with their free time.
You can also have a role about taking a break where you can go customize your house.

Overall, this game is really fun with friends and really relaxing to play
whenever you want to cool off.
