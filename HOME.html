Introduction

Roblox is an online gaming platform where users can play, create and share games made by other players. It offers many different types of experiences, such as adventure, role-playing, racing, battling and simulation. Roblox also allows users to customize their avatars and interact with friends in a virtual environment.

My Favorite Roblox Games

The Undead Coming

Play as a Survivor and fight against zombies in a round where
it progessively gets harder OR play as a zombie and defeat the survivors
before the time runs out.

Pilgrammed

Explore an expansive world full of mysteries yet to discover, level up your
character, join guilds from different places of the world and find out what
corrupts the world with evil.

Build a Boat for Treasure

Make a boat and make sure it reaches the end for a shiny prize. Once you're
rich enough, push the game's physics to its limits and build anything from a small
raft to a realistic vehicle.

The Battle Bricks

Battle against the Red team in this "The Battle Cats" inspired game
with unique mechanics further down the line.

Work at a Pizza Place

Choose a role in the pizza making restaurant and fullfill your customer's
orders in order to fill their appetite and also fill your wallet with money!
